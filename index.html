// 01 · WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR

People who build things to see if they work.

Students, graduates and experienced engineers are all welcome. What matters is that you've made something, broken it and made it better.

AI and machine learning

Teach a drone to fly itself, recognise what's on the ground below it and turn live footage into a clear account of what happened.

looking_for(["autonomy", "vision", "ML"])

Electronics and embedded

Flight controllers, firmware, power systems and radio links.

looking_for(["firmware", "PCB", "RF"])

Mechanical and robotics

Airframes, docks and the mechanisms that land, swap and relaunch a drone with no one on site.

looking_for(["CAD", "mechatronics"])

Flight and field

Drone technicians and pilots who test, install and keep the fleet flying.

looking_for(["testing", "maintenance"])
